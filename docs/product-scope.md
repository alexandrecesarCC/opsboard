# Product scope
Opsboard is a B2B operations workspace for projects, tasks, team coordination, schedules and operational metrics. Guest mode is an isolated in-memory interactive demonstration, not an anonymous authenticated account. No remote calls or durable guest records are required by its store. Paid registration is closed in the UI until a payment provider, verified webhook and server-side subscription entitlement gate are configured. This does not disable direct Supabase signup or enforce payment at the API; both must be addressed before public commercial launch. Email activation OTP requires confirmed Auth settings, email template and delivery configuration. Pricing, currency and provider remain undecided.
