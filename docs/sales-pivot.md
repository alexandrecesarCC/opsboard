# Sales operations pivot
Each inbound WhatsApp message is a durable pending work item, keyed by provider ID. Only an enabled, server-mapped phone number selects a tenant. Webhook verifies HMAC before parsing. Retries deduplicate via unique provider ID. The inbox requires AAL2 and membership. AI adapter is staged; a durable retry worker must invoke it and persist results. Reply sending, outbound confirmations, contact grouping, live polling, templates, catalog, orders and sales metrics remain pending. Never mark a request answered without confirmed outbound send; reconcile uncertain sends. Human approval is required; AI cannot promise stock or prices. Use approved Meta templates outside the permitted service window. Configure server-only META_APP_SECRET, META_VERIFY_TOKEN, SUPABASE_URL, SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY, OPENAI_API_KEY, OPENAI_MODEL. Never commit secrets. No integration is active until the authorized channel and external accounts are configured. Minimize customer data sent to AI; establish retention and privacy policy before launch. Existing project tools are retained temporarily during migration; not yet exclusive sales UX.
